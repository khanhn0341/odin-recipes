Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is one of the oldest and most beloved comfort foods in Italian cuisine. The classic dish consists of stacked layers of wide, flat pasta sheets alternating with rich fillings like savory ragù (meat sauce), creamy béchamel, and melted cheeses like mozzarella, ricotta, and Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Originating in regions like Emilia-Romagna, it is baked in an oven (al forno) until bubbly and golden brown. Today, countless regional and global variations exist—ranging from Neapolitan versions filled with mini meatballs to modern vegetarian adaptations packed with fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a skillet.
  2. Layer the noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  3. Bake at 375 degrees for 40 minutes.
