Chicken roast

Description

Roasted chicken is a timeless, comforting culinary staple prized for its golden, crispy skin and tender, juicy meat.

Whole-roasting a chicken seals in its natural flavors, while the simple combination of heat, fat, and aromatics transforms a basic bird into a deeply savory centerpiece perfect for Sunday dinners or meal prepping.

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken: 1.5 to 2 kg
  • Olive oil: 3-4 tablespoons
  • Aromatics (for the cavity):a small bunch of fresh rosemary
  • Seasoning:1 teaspoon garlic powder or paprika

Steps

  1. Prep and Dry the Chicken
  2. Season and Stuff
  3. Coat the Skin
  4. Roast
