Biryani

Description

Biryani is a celebrated, aromatic rice dish originating from the Indian subcontinent, known for its rich layers of long-grain basmati rice, heavily spiced meat (like chicken, mutton, or beef) or vegetables, and fragrant aromatics.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or meat: 1 kg
  • Yogurt: 1 cup
  • Ginger-garlic paste: 2 tablespoons
  • Basmati rice: 700g

Steps

  1. Marinate the Meat
  2. Prepare and Par-Boil the Rice
  3. Cook the Biryani Gravy (Korma)
  4. Layer and "Dum" (Steam)
